Meridian Run — Type Specimen 1

Direction locked (T2): condensed-techno. Three roles, candidate faces shown side-by-side with identical sample text so they compare directly. Display samples are rendered under both ends of the calm→climax arc (Vector Standard cyan, Polybius Dusk magenta) with neon glow. Informs the typography tokens in DESIGN.md — fontFamily / fontSize / fontWeight / lineHeight / letterSpacing. Final pick is the developer's.

Pairing options — presented, not decided

Chakra Petch  +  Inter  +  JetBrains Mono Rajdhani  +  Rubik  +  Share Tech Mono Coach lean: Chakra Petch + Inter + JetBrains Mono — Chakra Petch's squared apertures give the most "neon-sign / tactical-HUD" read of the three (Geometry-Wars vector, not sport-stripe), Inter carries menu/tooltips with the lowest visual noise at small sizes, and JetBrains Mono has the cleanest stable digit grid for score / XXs jitter-free counting. The Rajdhani + Share Tech Mono pairing is the warmer, more synthwave-arcade alternative if the identity skews Llamasoft over Resogun.
