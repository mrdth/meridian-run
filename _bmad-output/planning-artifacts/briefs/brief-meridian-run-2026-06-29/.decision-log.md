## 2026-06-29 — Vision: core fantasy resolved

- **Core fantasy = the Godhood leads.** Mrdth's gut pull is the build/godhood
  ("how OP can I become"); he self-identifies this as *his* playstyle and acknowledges
  it may not be universal. Resolution: **Godhood is the engine; the Gamble (courting
  capture, sacrifice) and the Test (skill/dodge) give it teeth and broaden appeal.**
  The brainstorming's power-escalation-as-hook resolution is HONORED — not re-litigated.
- **Crystallized one-sentence fantasy (proposed):**
  *"Build a ship into absurd, compounding power — then see how far your skill can carry
  your own greed."* Leads with the build, ends with the gamble (glass-cannon god).
- **Scope stance — "portfolio driver, but architected to grow":** Mrdth wants the MVP's
  foundation to support growth beyond MVP, even though finishing a complete lean game is
  the primary driver. Healthy if disciplined: ship a *finished* lean game (portfolio win)
  whose systems are **architecturally open / data-driven** so post-1.0 growth = adding
  data, not rewrites. Growth is a *possibility the architecture preserves*, not a
  commitment being made now.
- **Lean MVP proposed by Mrdth:** 2–3 ship types · handful of power-ups · 1 tier (~5 waves).
  Affirmed as smart — validates loop + build engine + cross-pollination *mechanism* in
  miniature.
- **Coaching pushbacks raised (to resolve in brief):**
  1. **Can ~5 waves *feel* the godhood?** The fantasy is *compounding* power reaching
     absurdity; a 5-wave run may not reach a peak without **power-curve compression**.
     MVP validity depends on feeling the fantasy, not just tracing it.
  2. **Godhood needs teeth.** MVP must include courting-capture + sacrifice + dodge-skill,
     not the build layer alone — godhood without risk is boring. So minimal = **build +
     gamble together**, not build alone.
  3. **Cross-pollination at 2–3 ships proves the *mechanism*, not the *depth*.** Be honest
     in the brief that the 50-hour combinatorial depth is deferred post-1.0.

## 2026-06-29 — Audience & positioning resolved (decisive)

- **Primary audience = roguelite build-crafters** (Brotato / Vampire Survivors / Hades /
  Isaac crowd). The Godhood fantasy is their love language; matches Mrdth's conviction.
- **Secondary audience = classic-arcade / shmup veterans** (Galaga nostalgists,
  score-attack). Smaller, loyal, the under-served "unclaimed territory." A bonus, not target.
- **KEY POSITIONING (Mrdth):** the shmup/Galaga aspect is a **DIFFERENTIATOR, not the
  identity.** Meridian Run = a build-crafter roguelite whose combat chassis is **1-axis
  fixed-screen Galaga** (vs Brotato/VS's Robotron twin-stick). The chassis swap is the
  edge; the 1-axis constraint is what makes it "not just another Brotato."
- **Differentiator (mechanical, not cosmetic):** (1) fresh 1-axis combat feel — weave
  fire-columns, constrained-lane timing — vs open twin-stick kiting; (2) a capture/rescue/
  sacrifice build economy the genre leaders lack (they are pure item-stacking); (3) the
  glass-cannon hitbox — risk teeth only 1-axis geometry makes brutal.
- **Elevator pitch (emerging):** *"Meridian Run is a build-crafter roguelite built on
  Galaga's 1-axis chassis — weave a single lane, court capture to fuel runaway builds, and
  see how far your greed can carry you."*
- **Honest risk to record:** the 1-axis chassis is double-edged — the wedge AND the known
  movement-depth risk (modern players expect more positional expressiveness). **Mitigation
  (already in brainstorming):** constrained movement makes build+gamble decisions the
  primary expression of skill/strategy — the design's central bet.
- **Art/audio leaning (resolved by audience):** build-crafter reward — juice, readability,
  clear power-up feedback, progression UI — over shmup-purist minimalism.

## 2026-06-29 — Content & direction resolved; drafting

- **Art:** clean geometric **neon-vector**. Chosen because it serves every constraint at
  once: achievable solo (prototype already used geometric shapes), maximizes 1-axis
  lane-readability (functionally essential for fire-column weaving), honors arcade vector
  lineage (Asteroids/Tempest/Geometry Wars/Resogun/Nova Drift), enables cheap juice
  (glow/particles/shake). Pixel art rejected as higher-cost + worse readability for a solo
  intermediate dev (no strong personal pull to override).
- **Audio:** synthwave / arcade-electronic (fits neon-vector, Galaga's electronic roots,
  power-escalation energy); punchy SFX carry juice alongside visuals.
- **Narrative:** LIGHT — espionage reframe (captured ships hack enemy mainframe → return
  with ship specs) justifies fleet unlocks; "Meridian" = line-through-poles (1-axis nod) +
  peak/ascender. Flavor, not load-bearing; no deep worldbuilding for v1.0.
- **All seven brief sections now grounded in decisions.** Drafting `brief.md` (1–2 pp) +
  `addendum.md` (scope milestones + pointers to deeper source docs). Content-breadth lists
  remain deferred (separate post-systems brainstorm), stated as orders of magnitude only.
